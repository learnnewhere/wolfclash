FULL DESCRIPTION  (1488/4000 characters)
Run, hunt and grow in a moonlit forest arena where bigger wolves are always on your trail.

Start as a small wolf. Eat meat, catch rabbits and pounce on smaller wolves to grow stronger. Every wolf shows its power above its head: green ones you can eat, red ones will eat you. Dodge the hunters, use your dash to escape and keep growing.

GROW EXTRA HEADS
As your wolf gets bigger it sprouts new heads, up to five. Every new head gives you:
• A stronger bite, so you can take down wolves bigger than you
• An extra heart to survive more bites
• A little more speed

SURVIVE THE HUNT
More hunters join the chase the longer you last. Red arrows at the edge of the screen warn you where danger is coming from. Lose all your hearts and you're knocked out.

CLASH THE ALPHA
When you're knocked out, your wolf charges the giant Alpha boss. Every bit of your size hits the boss, and every head multiplies the damage. Defeat the Alpha for a big bonus and face a tougher one next time.

FEATURES
• Simple one-finger controls: touch and drag anywhere to run
• Dash button for quick escapes
• Grow from one head to five heads
• Alpha boss fights with levels that get harder
• Score for survival time, food, wolves eaten and boss damage
• Your best score is saved on your phone

Wolf Clash Arena needs an internet connection to play. The game is free and contains ads, including optional reward ads that let you revive or double your boss score.
